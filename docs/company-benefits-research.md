## Offer Evaluation Checklist

Ask these questions before accepting any role:

- What is the normal working hour pattern for this team?
- How often does the team work overtime near release dates?
- Is there on-call? If yes, how often and how is it compensated?
- How many cross-timezone meetings are expected each week?
- Is the role hybrid, remote, or fixed office?
- How much English communication is used in daily work?
- What is the annual leave policy and can it actually be used?
- How are performance reviews and promotions handled?
- Is the salary base-heavy, bonus-heavy, or dependent on uncertain incentives?
- Is the team growing, maintaining, or under reorganization?
